# Frozen bottle/table category transfer — 4 October 2026

This is a small out-of-category stress test for the working RGB-to-3D pipeline. The [six-scene selection](results/sunrgbd_bottle_table6_selection.json) and [evaluation protocol](results/sunrgbd_bottle_table6_protocol.json) were saved before viewing images, depth, or 3D references. The selection used official SUN RGB-D test metadata, required one annotated 2D/3D bottle and table, excluded prior project selections with a 30-index gap, and includes six distinct recorded sequences. Index separation does not prove room independence. One RGB-only review then saved [three positive and three negative annotated bottle/table relations](results/sunrgbd_bottle_table6_rgb_labels.json), including the visible alternate surfaces in negative scenes. The [RGB contact sheet](outputs/sunrgbd_bottle_table6_rgb_sheet.png) shows the paired boxes.

Grounding DINO Tiny was prompted separately for `bottle` and `table` at box/text thresholds 0.25. SAM ViT-B segmented the detected boxes, Depth Anything V2 Metric Hypersim Small predicted depth, and official intrinsics lifted detections to 3D. The unchanged box-foot/depth-gap proposal, visible mask adjacency gate, and 0.4 m child-only camera-ray correction were applied without tuning on these six scenes. Detection matching against annotated 2D boxes (class-aware IoU ≥ 0.5) was used only for scoring. The [per-scene output](results/sunrgbd_bottle_table6_transfer.json) contains raw detections, proposals, mask evidence, and center errors.

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---:|
| Both annotated objects detected and matched | 4/6 scenes |
| Visibly positive annotated pair proposed before / after mask gate | 2/3 / 2/3 |
| Visibly negative annotated pair proposed before / after mask gate | 0/3 / 0/3 |
| Positive matched bottle center error, box | 0.748 m mean, 2 scenes |
| Positive matched bottle center error, SAM mask | 0.652 m mean, 2 scenes |
| Positive matched bottle center error, SAM + fixed correction | 0.486 m mean, 2 scenes |
| Positive matched table center error, box / SAM | 0.735 / 0.464 m mean, 2 scenes |

Both corrected positive bottles improved: `sunrgbd_000057` from 0.886 to 0.744 m and `sunrgbd_002874` from 0.419 to 0.227 m. This is a **two-pair descriptive result**, not evidence that the cup-derived 0.4 m offset is generally appropriate for bottles. The third positive, `sunrgbd_000451`, did not have a matched detector pair and contributes to the 2/3 coverage denominator, not the error means.

The three annotated-pair negatives expose different cases. In `sunrgbd_003776`, the bottle sits on a right laboratory counter while the annotated table is a separate left surface. In `sunrgbd_003937`, the bottle is on an upper shelf rather than the annotated lower worktable. Neither image produced a paired proposal. In `sunrgbd_004139`, the annotated bottle is on a rear kitchen counter and the annotated table is in front. The detector matched the foreground table but did not match the annotated bottle; it found two other bottle candidates and generated two contact-gated proposals. Their physical validity is unknown from the paired annotation, and the single-pair correction abstained because two candidates survived. Thus 0/3 is **paired-annotation specificity**, while 1/3 negative scenes still contained some proposal. Whole-scene precision requires exhaustive support-object labeling.

Together with the earlier [laptop/table transfer](RESEARCH_STATUS_2026-10-02_LAPTOP_TRANSFER.md), the fixed correction improved all seven positive annotated pairs with matched detections and a unique candidate across these two tiny selected sets (five laptops, two bottles). This observation is encouraging but has substantial selection, category, and annotation limits. The two tests should not be treated as a benchmark or paper-level novelty evidence. The next research step is an independently frozen sample with all plausible parent surfaces labeled, hard negatives without alternative support candidates, and comparison to a size-aware or uncertainty-aware correction specified before seeing its 3D references.

A [post-hoc reference-gap audit](results/sunrgbd_laptop_bottle_true_gap_diagnostic.json) found mean annotated child/table center-height gaps of 0.424 m for five positive laptops and 0.312 m for three positive bottles. Individual bottle gaps ranged from 0.077 to 0.458 m. This explains why a universal 0.4 m offset is a fragile geometric assumption even though it improved center error in the two corrected bottle cases. These are ground-truth values inspected **after** the frozen test and must not be used to tune that test retroactively.

The production CLI now accepts `--grounding-labels` and `--support-child-labels`, so bottle and laptop support proposals use the same end-to-end path as cups. A [positive bottle layout](outputs/bottle_table6_cli_000057.json) reproduced the frozen evaluator's −0.14187 m camera-ray shift exactly. On [scene 004139](outputs/bottle_table6_cli_004139.json), two contact-gated proposals survived; the CLI recorded `abstained_no_unique_visible_contact_proposal` and did not apply a correction. These two CLI checks establish implementation consistency, not additional independent evidence.
